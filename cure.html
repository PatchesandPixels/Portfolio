Graphic Design  ·  Motion  ·  CURE Auto Insurance

Loading animations, email campaigns,
and mobile UI for a legacy insurance brand.

The brief

Bring warmth to a legacy insurer's least-loved moments — wait screens, emails, and mobile UI — so the brand feels human, not corporate.

The constraints

A conservative brand system, real production surfaces (policy binding, app-download campaigns), and zero tolerance for performance cost in the app.

Key decisions

Built every animation in Lottie instead of GIF or video so they ship at kilobytes and stay crisp at any size; used the wait moment to tell a small neighbourhood story; explored multiple colour systems before locking the campaign look.
