The brief

Design UI screens, illustrated characters, and promotional graphics across a family of online gaming brands — Winloot, Sweeps.pet, RewardIt, and Winstakes.

The constraints

A high-energy sweepstakes audience, fast campaign cycles, and four brands that each needed their own personality without four separate pipelines.

Key decisions

Built reusable layout systems (lobbies, pick-entry, prize screens) that reskin per brand; leaned on illustrated characters to give promos warmth; spun seasonal variants like the NFL Pick 8 from one master layout.
