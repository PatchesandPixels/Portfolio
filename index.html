About

Andy Nguyen

I'm a product and UX/UI designer in Seattle. I studied Digital Technology & Culture at Washington State, and I've spent the years since chasing one feeling: the moment a product stops feeling like software and starts feeling effortless.

I build from the ground up, guided by a simple idea — design for people, not personas. The same care goes into a two-sided marketplace, an insurance flow, or a thousand small-business apps: strip away the friction until what's left is obvious.

I don't treat product design and graphic design as separate crafts. The eye that structures a booking flow also art-directs a scratchcard or a gaming UI. And yes — all of it is thoroughly cat-approved.

The garden this site is built on

枯山水

The dry garden craft

Raked sand and standing stones. The stones are permanence; the raked lines, redrawn constantly, are thought itself; the empty space between them — ma — matters as much as the objects. Disciplined iteration, and the restraint to let clarity breathe.

鯉

The koi pond outcomes

The living counterpart to the dry garden. In the old story a carp swims up a waterfall and becomes a dragon — perseverance and transformation through effort. I judge my work by what it changes, not just how it looks.

一期一会

The tea ceremony presence

Total, unhurried attention to the person in front of you — ichigo ichie, "one time, one meeting." The posture I bring to research sessions and to teams.
